The full license is retained at [OFL.txt](../src/Beep.SimpleDraw.Core/Fonts/OFL.txt) and published at fonts/OFL.txt. Source: [Google Fonts Kalam-Regular.ttf](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/kalam/Kalam-Regular.ttf), retrieved 2026-10-05. SHA256: `57cecb63d4608019371954274ae1d8c397764debd5b19d4a33c1efa4dc923c0b`.

Forty-five groups cover Web dependency candidates, Beep.Skia, Kalam, Mono/ICU and Emscripten. Full vendor collections are retained conservatively; their presence does not assert every described subcomponent is linked.
